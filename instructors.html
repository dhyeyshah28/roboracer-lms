Teach Roboracer at your university

Copy the complete course without licensing, add your university or lab logo and colours, and choose the lectures that fit your students. Here is how it works, step by step.

  • Write an email with your university, names and what you are interested in
  • You receive an account as a (restricted) admin for the LMS
  • Short tour: the Courses portal (what students see) and the Studio portal (where you edit)
  • Enter the necessary information in Studio
  • Set the start and end date of the course
  • Integrate your university logos
  • Add instructors and their images
  • Attach your specific course syllabus
  • Add new team members to the course
  • Open your course in Studio
  • Delete course material you do not need
  • Create a new unit
  • Create text material
  • Add slides and videos
  • Add advanced problem types, e.g. quizzes
  • Publish the new material
  • Manage everything from the Courses portal
  • Students need to sign in first
  • Invite students by link
  • Students enroll in the specific course
  • What students see and how they go through the course
  • Interaction: asking questions and taking part in discussions
  • How students hand in material

For Roboracer team admins only.

  • Sign in to the Courses portal and open the Django admin console
  • Go to Authentication and Authorization › Users
  • Click Add user and enter the user information
  • Open the new user: set the email address and make the account active
  • Mark as Staff — only for Roboracer team members. Afterwards Studio login is possible
  • Open Studio and go to the desired course
  • Settings dropdown → Course Team
  • Click “Add a new team member”, enter the email address
  • Make them staff for the course
  • Go back to Studio as admin
  • Re-run the desired 4-, 10- or 15-week course by clicking on it
  • Rename the course and add the correct affiliation
  • Add the new user to the course as Staff
  • The user can now see the course and add material
